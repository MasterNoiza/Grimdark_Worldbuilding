# Overview 

A extremely zealous order of Knights, mostly confined to [[1. The Compendium/002. Atlas/002. The Northern Expanse/1. Overview\|the Northern Expanse]] and its neighboring territories.

In ancient times the order was founded on principles of compassion, altruism and hope as a direct result to the catastrophe known as [[The Convergence]], despite all odds attempting to retrieve essential knowledge, relics and most importantly, save as many souls from suffering a gruesome death as possible.

Over the centuries, millennia and aeons however, the Order has shifted and evolved significantly. Growing ever more zealous, harboring increasing hatred for the very thing that caused the near-destrucion of the world: Weaving, mutations & corruption in all shapes and forms.
