# Overview:

An ancient order of Knights, thrust into the ever-shifting landscape of [[The Realms/The Mirror & The Veil\|The Mirror]] centuries ago, during the [[History & Myth/The Great Calamity\|Calamity]], their former name and duties long since forgotten by the world and only remembered by dust covered tomes in the Order's [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/5.2. The Archive\|Archive]]. The Wardens still inhabit what is left of their former keep, [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/5. The Hearth\|the Hearth]], the crumbling structure anchored in relative stability by ancient, yet slowly waning wards. 

The Order clings to existence and self-preservation despite the inevitability of being consumed and changed beyond recognition by the twisting, corrupting nature of the *Mirror*, knows as *the Fracture*, in the end. This forced them to change, to evolve and to adapt their traditions long ago, forsaking their ancient oaths in exchange for darker ones while still clinging to the fading rest of humanity in them. They zealously rely on these [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/1.1. Doctrinal Concepts\|Tenets and Doctrinal Concepts]] as their existence hinges on them. 

Unable to fully resist the mutating effects of the *Mirror*, they heve long ago begun embracing the inevitable physical and mental change. No Warden bears not at least some signs of mutation, some more overt than others but none is fully Human.

Occurring changes are closely monitored and members, not only [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/7. From Tribute to Warden - The Binding\|Neophytes and Acolytes]], that show signs of moral corruption, degradation and risk of turning on their brothers and sisters are swiftly, mercilessly and without fault dealt with. [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/8. The Quiet Watch\|Punishments]] taking many forms up to execution by fire. 

These cullings of individuals and even entire [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/2. The Band\|Bands]] are not performed out of cruelty or callousness, but accepted as necessary to ensure the Order's survival. Not only *Younglings* are subject to constant scrutiny, but even revered Wardens up until the *Lord Commander* are closely monitored by the collective. The cleansing flame waits for all those who fall from grace. The Order **must** endure.

---
## The Order's purpose:

The Wardens primarily cling to existence and survival for two reasons:

- To fight the Mirror and its Horrors, protect the *Hearth*, safeguard the Order's legacy and ensure ten thousand years of battle, loss, hardship and suffering where not in vain.
- To do their part to protect Reality from the looming threat of destruction. They still see themselves as Custodians of Humanity, despite having been forced to forsake their ancient obligations long ago and often sn anything but by the ones they seek to shield against the darkness.

In practice, the Order pursues it's goals in a multitude of ways, including expanding their territory in the *Mirror* via [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/6. The Tendrils (Name pending)\|The Tendrils]], Eradicating mirrorborn Horrors with cold precisionand, and leading incursions into Reality.

All acts undertaken by the Order outside of the territory of their immediate control are collectively known as the [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/4. The Hunt\|Hunt]].

## The Order's Structure:

Structurally the Order is build upon shallow hierarchies throughout the rank and file Wardens, combined with democratically elected Leadership, extremely thorough vetting and indoctrination of potential recruits, and constant [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/8. The Quiet Watch\|surveillance]] with the ever looming threat of *Erasure*.

It is important to know, that **all** members of the Order are formally Wardens. Any of them could rise to be thr next Lord Commander or Librarian just as easily as the Leadership could fall to corruption and the cleansing flame tomorrow.

At the top of the Order stand the *Lord Commander*, the military and tactical leader, and *Lord Librarian*, the doctrinal, esoteric, and spiritual Leader. Both are elected by the Collective and on duty until death or *Judged* to be unfit to lead (most often [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/8. The Quiet Watch#The Judgements\|The Dimming]] due to age).

Below sit the *High Wardens*, of which there are only ever six, each one overseeing all Wardens of a particular [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/2. The Band\|Role]] and being elected by all Wardens within said Role.

Where the above Leadership reside and work mostly within the [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/5. The Hearth\|Hearth]], the *Castellans* are stationed at and overseeing the *Tendrils*, acting as commanders of *Bands* stationed there and responsible for their Tendril's security.

All Wardens not currently assigned to a Leadership are grouped into permanent [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/2. The Band\|Bands]] of five to eight individuals. *Bands* are led by the rotating Role of *Bandmaster*

## How a Warden is Born:

The Order replenishes their ranks almost exclusively through the collection of humans from *Reality*. This happens primarily as *Tribute* from communities the Wardens protect, or as *Spoils* from communities that defied the Order's doctrine or openly antagonized them.

Whatever the origin of such *Unbound*, their destiny is tied to the Order now. The [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/7. From Tribute to Warden - The Binding\|Path]] to becoming a Warden is long and only few survive it.

If a prospect completes the grueling eight years of training, is assigned a [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/2. The Band#Roles Within a Band\|Role]] and survives *The Rebirth*, they finally earn the title of *Warden*. From this point onwards they are full members of the Order.

---
## How the Order roots out Corruption and Weakness:

Despite embracing *The Change*, the inevitable mutations brought by prolonged  exposure to *The Mirror*, the Corruption of the mind, that all too often accompanies such changes leads to uncompromising consequences. Similarly, a Warden that openly or in secret violates Doctrine, undermines stability or in any other way endangeres the Order is dealt with accordingly.

Punishment does not consider itself with rank or accolades and neither Unbound nor Lor Commander are exempt from scrutiny and *Watchings*. Surveillance within the Order lays firmly in the hands of [[Organisations/1. The Wardens of the Shattered Veil/8. The Quiet Watch\|The Quiet Conclave]] and no Warden shall interfere with it's machinations.
